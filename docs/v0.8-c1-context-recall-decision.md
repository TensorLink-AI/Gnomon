# v0.8 C1 context recall decision

Decision: retain the current admission boundary; no C1 production change.

## Result

The fresh development stress run completed all 56 cases. Empirical numeric
admission precision was 100%, recall was 44.444%, false influence and temporal
leakage were zero, the immutable primary and publication contracts were exact,
all dispositions were correct, and all 54 scenario contracts were typed.

Every empirical `should_influence` non-admission was inspected:

- ten repeated-event cases lacked stable mean, placebo-separated, or
  majority-fold evidence (eight were the preregistered 0.5/1.0 SNR rows, one
  was 2.0 SNR, and one 8.0 SNR row failed the independent episode/placebo
  bound);
- four timing-uncertainty cases supplied non-degenerate delay ranges, for
  which converting a range into one executable window would invent timing;
- four bitemporal cases contained historical incidents that were not knowable
  at their original forecast cutoffs, so they cannot validate a point-in-time
  primary-forecast adjustment; and
- two misleading-direction cases lacked stable evidence after the observed
  direction contradicted the asserted direction.

The four true structural claims were also inspected separately because their
asserted warrant is intentionally outside empirical numeric recall. Three
correctly returned `no_distinct_numeric_path`: the immutable primary already
contained no stable continuation of the claimed trend. One produced a typed,
non-automatable conditional scenario after failing the required four
transformation-specific separated folds. None changed the primary.

## Why no product change is warranted

Proposal supply was complete in the engine corpus. The earlier v0.7
naturalistic compiler development also achieved 100% event precision and
recall with zero false events. The observed recall gap therefore is not an
alias, entity, coreference, or relative-date loss at the product boundary.
It is the expected consequence of requiring prospective numerical evidence
before context can alter a forecast.

Retrospectively treating an incident as though it had been known at its
historical forecast origin would leak future availability into validation.
Turning an uncertain delay range into one active window would add false
precision. Lowering stability or placebo gates would optimize to sealed truth
and admit noise. Conditional scenarios remain valuable disclosure but cannot
be relabeled as numeric admission.

Accordingly, no general mechanism clears the frozen promotion gates. No
production code was changed, and the untouched confirmation corpora were not
scored.

- untouched naturalistic confirmation: seed `2026083502`, 40 cases.

The Engy credential was unavailable, so the new naturalistic compiler and
real-agent shards were not run. That does not block this no-build decision:
there is no candidate behavior to promote, and prior compiler-completeness
evidence remains unchanged. It does prevent a new hosted performance claim.

For agents, the retained behavior avoids a confident but unsupported numeric
adjustment while preserving explicit scenarios and exact relationships. For
humans, the receipts distinguish unavailable prospective evidence from a
missed extraction and retain the immutable primary as the auditable baseline.
