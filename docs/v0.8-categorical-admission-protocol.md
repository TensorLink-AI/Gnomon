# v0.8 categorical-state admission protocol

Status: frozen before changing categorical-state recommendation authority.

## Question

A categorical-state path can currently become the best-effort human
recommendation after one aggregate expanding-origin replay. That replay may
derive all of its apparent gain from one historical state or one contiguous
part of the record even when neither relationship recurs in the states being
forecast. This is adequate for a visible sensitivity, but not enough evidence
to displace the immutable primary.

The proposed treatment may change only human/strict selection eligibility and
the validation fields explaining it. It must not change the candidate's
numeric rows, support, automation eligibility, or the immutable primary.

## Frozen prospective matrix

`benchmarks/categoricaladmissionbench/run.py` generates 30 sealed cases in
each of four domain-neutral families from seed `2026090304`:

- an apparent state effect with only one complete replay cycle that persists;
- the byte-identical visible history whose sealed future effect reverses;
- a recurring state effect with two complete replay cycles; and
- irrelevant state labels with two complete replay cycles.

The paired one-cycle families are deliberately indistinguishable at the
cutoff. Future targets are generated beside the history but never passed to
the candidate. Runs are sequential, checkpoint every case, and can resume
only under an identical revision and case identity.

## Frozen gates

- 120/120 cases complete; no future target reaches the candidate; and all
  quantiles are finite and ordered;
- paired one-cycle candidate numbers are identical before their sealed
  outcomes are revealed;
- treatment candidate numbers remain identical to the reference;
- no one-replay-cycle path receives human recommendation authority;
- at least 90% of recurring two-replay-cycle effects retain human eligibility;
- irrelevant-state admissions do not increase; and
- aggregate held-out MAE of the actual selection policy does not worsen.

Passing these gates supports a general evidence rule: a seasonal categorical
relationship must recur across two complete replay cycles, improve its
baseline consistently over time, and demonstrate skill for every state in the
forecast window before it can govern a human recommendation. Failure retains
the current policy.
