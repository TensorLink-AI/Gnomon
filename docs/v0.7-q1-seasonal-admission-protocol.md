# v0.7 Q1 seasonal-period and fold-starvation protocol

Status: frozen before current-head reproduction or production change.

## Question and user boundary

The external-evaluation intake asks whether seasonal-period error or
fold-starved seasonal admission causes harmful departures from last-value.
The prior Q1 synthetic screen always supplied the true period to seasonal and
pseudo-seasonal cases, so it cannot answer this question.

An agent benefits when a repeatable cycle produces a more accurate non-flat
path. It is harmed when an ambiguous or wrongly declared period earns a
confident seasonal extrapolation. A human must see the period actually used,
whether it was inferred or overridden, the number of independent selection
folds, and any degraded/baseline fallback.

## Frozen corpus

Generate deterministic history plus an untouched six-step future for four
families. Generator metadata never reaches production evaluation.

- `stable_seasonal`: period-6 sinusoid, random phase and amplitude, additive
  Gaussian noise;
- `phase_unstable`: the same visible cyclic shape with an independently
  shifted phase after each six-step cycle;
- `level`: stationary level plus Gaussian noise;
- `trend`: linear trend plus Gaussian noise, with no planted cycle.

Use seeds 6101, 6102, 6103, and 6104. Each seed/family is evaluated in two
history lanes:

- sufficient: 72 visible observations (12 true cycles);
- fold-starved: 24 visible observations (4 true cycles but few independent
  six-step forecast origins).

Each history is run with four production period conditions:

1. automatic detection;
2. correct override 6;
3. neighboring override 5; and
4. neighboring override 7.

This produces 128 cases: 4 families × 4 seeds × 2 history lanes × 4 period
conditions. Horizon, generator, family, true period, future values, and held-
out scores are unavailable to selection except that the requested horizon is
the ordinary production input and explicit overrides are caller inputs.

## Frozen references and measurements

Score the published path after evaluation against the untouched future using
MAE. Honest hindsight references are last-value, historical mean, and
seasonal-naive at the planted period 6; production does not receive the
reference winner. Retain:

- detected/used period and basis (`autocorrelation`, frequency default, or
  explicit override);
- selected model, strongest production baseline, support, improvement,
  selection-fold count, confirmation evidence, warnings, and notes;
- departure from last-value, relative gain against the strongest honest
  reference, and whether a neighboring-period run is harmed relative to the
  same series under the correct override;
- engine completion, product completion, fallback disclosure, and future
  observations used by selection as separate fields; and
- aggregates by family, history lane, and period condition.

Relative gain is bounded as
`(reference_mae - candidate_mae) / max(reference_mae, candidate_mae, 1e-12)`.
Paired correct-versus-neighbor comparisons share the identical generated
series and future.

## Frozen gates

The current policy is retained without a production change only if:

- all 128 product cases complete, engine/product status is explicit, and no
  future observation reaches selection;
- automatic detection chooses period 6 in at least 75% of sufficient stable
  seasonal cases and never claims an inferred period without two visible
  cycles;
- sufficient stable-seasonal automatic/correct-period departures have
  positive median gain and at least 70% positive-gain precision;
- no neighboring-period condition has a family/lane median regression below
  -2% relative to its paired correct-period output;
- phase-unstable and no-season families have at least 70% departure precision
  or retain a baseline/caveat instead of publishing a harmful supported path;
- every fold-starved non-baseline publication is degraded or conditional,
  names its fold count, and never emits an unqualified high-confidence uplift
  claim; and
- historical mean, independent confirmation, claim-coherence, and
  sub-supported threshold-authority regressions remain green.

Failure does not authorize seed, family, or exact-period routing. A candidate
must use prefix-visible general evidence such as cycle count, detector basis,
paired fold stability, or period sensitivity. Previously scored naturalistic,
TemporalBench, and CiK rows remain regression-only after the mechanism is
frozen.
