# v0.7 Q1 fresh naturalistic confirmation protocol

Status: frozen before scoring or production implementation.

## Question and user boundary

The synthetic Q1 screen showed that Gnomon's production selector can make
useful selective departures from last-value. This confirmation asks whether
the unchanged selector does so on measured real-world series when judged
against an honest reference set that includes historical mean.

An agent benefits only when a non-flat path is more accurate than a simple
forecast it could have produced without Gnomon. A human must be able to tell
whether the claimed improvement survives stronger baseline choice, whether
the engine actually supported the path, and whether one source series is
dominating a nominally larger set of rolling-origin cases.

## Frozen corpus

The corpus contains 12 real observational series already vendored in the
repository, but not used in the prior TemporalBench/CiK forecast scoring or
the synthetic Q1 selector screen:

- four MIT-derived `facebook/prophet` example series under
  `benchmarks/breachbench/data/`: real Wikipedia traffic, Yosemite
  temperatures, Melbourne pedestrian counts, and US monthly retail sales;
- eight BSD-redistributed `statsmodels` series under
  `benchmarks/dossierbench/data/`: Mauna Loa CO2, El Nino SST, Nile flow,
  sunspots, US real GDP, unemployment, CPI, and M1.

Their source descriptions and licenses are retained in each directory's
`README.md`. Dataset identity is the committed path plus SHA-256 digest. The
values are not sent to an LLM and dataset names, paths, groups, case IDs, and
future outcomes never reach production evaluation.

Each series contributes four expanding-origin cases. Cutoff indices are
`floor(n * f)` for frozen fractions 0.60, 0.70, 0.80, and 0.90. Short and
long horizons alternate by origin using the per-series mapping in
`benchmarks/modelbench/run_naturalistic_selector.py`. The future begins at
the cutoff and is sliced away before the production `evaluate()` call. TSFM
discovery is disabled so machine-local model installations cannot change the
arm. Minimum improvement remains the production default 0.02.

The 48 cases are grouped only for reporting:

- operational: pedestrian, retail, sensor-temperature, and web-traffic;
- environmental: CO2 and El Nino;
- macro: GDP, unemployment, CPI, and M1;
- historical: Nile and sunspots.

These group labels are benchmark metadata only.

## Frozen references and metrics

Every held-out horizon is scored by mean absolute error for:

1. last-value;
2. seasonal-naive using the declared period; and
3. historical mean of the visible prefix.

The strongest reference is the lowest held-out MAE among those three. It is
chosen only for scoring after production evaluation and never fed back to the
selector. Product publication completion and engine support remain separate;
a disclosed best-effort last-value path counts as a completed product answer,
not an engine-supported selection.

A departure is any published path that differs numerically from last-value.
Its bounded relative gain is
`(reference_mae - candidate_mae) / max(reference_mae, candidate_mae, 1e-12)`.
Admission precision is the fraction of departures with positive gain against
the strongest reference. A departure is specifically mean-invalidated when
historical mean has lower MAE than the published path.

Uncertainty is a deterministic 4,000-draw cluster bootstrap of the median
departure gain, resampling source-series identities rather than pretending
four origins from one series are independent. Seed: 92742.

## Promotion and diagnostic gates

The unchanged policy is confirmed only if:

- all 48 product cases complete and every fallback is disclosed;
- zero future observations reach production evaluation;
- all three references are finite and explicitly measured;
- at least 10 departures occur;
- departure precision against the strongest reference is at least 70%;
- departure wins outnumber losses and median gain is positive;
- the 90% cluster-bootstrap lower bound is non-negative;
- every reporting group has median all-case gain of at least -2%;
- selection provenance and support are complete; and
- no departure is invalidated by historical mean.

Failure does not authorize tuning to dataset or case identity. If historical
mean invalidates departures, the next bounded candidate is to add a general
historical-mean mandatory baseline and rerun the same frozen cases. If the
reference set is sound but naturalistic precision or uncertainty fails, the
current selector is retained as unconfirmed and Q1 investigates admission or
abstention using prefix-visible evidence only. Previously scored
TemporalBench and CiK rows remain regression-only.
