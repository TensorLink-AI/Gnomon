# v0.8 C1 context recall at fixed safety

Status: frozen before fresh corpus generation, baseline execution, or C1
production changes.

## User boundary

Agents should find more genuinely usable, prefix-visible context without
inventing an effect size, causal path, entity match, or effective time. Humans
should receive an exact typed account of whether evidence was proposed,
numerically admitted, retained as a conditional scenario, or rejected. A
scenario is useful disclosure but is never counted as numeric admission.

The immutable primary forecast remains authoritative. In particular, an
asserted structural change that the primary already reflects has relationship
`no_distinct_numeric_path`; improving recall must not convert that relationship
into an applied adjustment.

## Frozen corpora and execution

Generate only after this protocol is committed:

- stress development: seed `2026083401`, four cases per stratum (56 cases);
- untouched stress confirmation: seed `2026083402`, eight cases per stratum
  (112 cases);
- naturalistic development: seed `2026083501`, four cases per family;
- untouched naturalistic confirmation: seed `2026083502`, ten cases per
  family.

The existing generators and oracle definitions are unchanged. The stress
surface is the deterministic engine and is run serially with durable per-row
checkpoints and `--resume`. Naturalistic compilation and bounded agent shards
use Evidence with DeepSeek `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` through Engy, temperature
zero, `jobs=1`, a 180-second request timeout, a 120-second tool timeout, at
most two retries, resumable atomic rows, and an explicit wall-clock bound.
Missing rows remain in denominators. If the Engy credential is unavailable,
those gates remain externally pending rather than being replaced by another
provider or local deterministic rows.

Seeds, case IDs, generator families, oracle dispositions, sealed futures,
answer choices, source identities, and benchmark labels may not enter product
behavior.

## Baseline diagnosis

Before a C1 implementation, inspect every development `should_influence`
non-admission by:

- compiler proposal supply and source/known-time completeness;
- entity, alias, coreference, relative-date, status, and scope resolution;
- typed event class, gate code, repeat folds, placebo separation, interval
  evidence, and effect timing;
- typed disposition and relationship to the immutable primary; and
- conditional-scenario availability and consequence completeness.

A candidate is permitted only when a general product boundary loses valid
prefix-visible evidence. Threshold reduction, oracle-conditioned routing,
invented magnitudes, inferred future knowledge, and counting scenario yield as
admission recall are prohibited. If all misses are evidence insufficiency,
the result is a no-build decision.

## Measures

Report matched denominators and confidence intervals where defined for:

- compiler proposal/event precision, recall, false events, invalid events,
  source completeness, and known-time completeness;
- numeric admission precision and recall;
- conditional-scenario recall and consequence completeness, separately;
- false influence, harmful admissions, leakage, publication parity, and
  immutable-primary behavior;
- typed disposition, relationship, and automation-eligibility completeness;
- final-agent preservation of every canonical typed relationship; and
- compiler, engine, tool, final-agent, request, transport-attempt, retry,
  token, latency, and provider-error accounting.

## Promotion and kill gates

Promotion requires the exact candidate to show on development and untouched
confirmation:

- 100% empirical numeric admission precision, zero false influence, and zero
  temporal leakage;
- exact publication parity, canonical-primary immutability, disposition,
  typed relationship, and automation eligibility;
- a positive one-sided exact McNemar result at `p < 0.05` or a strictly
  positive 95% paired-bootstrap lower bound for matched numeric admission
  recall over `should_influence` rows;
- no family recall regression greater than two percentage points;
- compiler precision at least 0.99, recall at least 0.95, zero false events,
  and no compiler regression;
- complete typed scenario consequences without using scenarios to inflate
  admission recall;
- exact relationship preservation in a bounded real-agent shard; and
- focused tests plus the full suite isolated from machine-local TSFM supply.

Kill the candidate immediately if it causes any leakage, false influence,
primary mutation, support/automation inflation, or typed-relationship loss.
Also kill it when development cannot produce a statistically admissible
recall mechanism without relaxing the existing evidence boundary. Preserve
all negative results and leave rejected mechanisms disabled or absent.
