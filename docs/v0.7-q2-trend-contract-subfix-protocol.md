# v0.7 Q2 trend contract subfix protocol

Status: frozen after the exact current-head baseline at `b764474`, before
production implementation or candidate scoring.

## Baseline diagnosis

TrendAnswerBench completed all 152 product cases with zero future use, exact
input/primary immutability, and no automation-eligible weak answer. Direction
accuracy on the 128 identifiable futures was 64.0625%. All 152 answers were
weak because a 48-step horizon over 240 observations supplies only two
disjoint rolling origins under the existing minimum-history rule. The
minimum-fold support threshold is therefore doing its job and is not loosened.

Three general typed-answer defects were visible in actual responses:

- 33 canonical directions disagreed with the label implied by the canonical
  numeric estimate. The executable returned the modal calibrated label while
  publishing the current point estimate as if they were one conclusion.
- all 24 insufficient-cycle seasonal cases returned a numeric `constant`
  answer with weak support, although the reasoning packet independently
  marked the requested trend unidentifiable;
- every weak two-fold result published a zero-width interval equal to its
  estimate, producing 0% held-out interval coverage and visually implying
  certainty that the support state explicitly denied; and
- the trend target was computed from the raw slope of a forecast path, so a
  partial seasonal arc could be mistaken for underlying drift even when the
  period was admitted.

The initial runner also required a finite estimate for every complete row and
treated an `uncertain` abstention as direction/slope disagreement. That is
inconsistent with the frozen protocol's required typed abstention. Correct
the harness before candidate work, rerun the unchanged baseline on an exact
commit, and retain both artifacts. This scorer correction cannot improve a
current baseline row because the baseline emits no abstentions.

## Frozen candidate

For the generic fitted `trend` executable only:

- compute the trend property with phase fixed effects when an admitted period
  has at least two complete cycles in the evaluated path;
- keep the existing prefix-only innovation scaling, candidate set, fold
  schedule, skill gate, support threshold, and primary-forecast boundary;
- make the canonical direction the label of the canonical point estimate;
  a different modal calibration label is diagnostic disagreement and cannot
  earn supported publication;
- when at least one calibration error exists but fewer than the support
  minimum, publish a conservative observed-error envelope rather than a
  zero-width interval, while retaining weak support;
- when an admitted seasonal adjustment is not identifiable from at least two
  visible cycles, return `direction: uncertain`, a null estimate/interval,
  `support: abstained`, and a typed reason; and
- when visible-history autocorrelation strongly detects a period that was not
  admitted (`season=1`), do not silently change the requested model. Record
  the detected period as unmodelled seasonal evidence and prohibit supported
  automation; if trend direction is not robust to seasonal adjustment,
  abstain rather than substitute a raw slope.

No rule may inspect benchmark family, seed, structural slope, realized future,
or expected direction. An explicit admitted period remains caller-authorized;
an automatically detected unadmitted period is evidence for qualification,
not permission to mutate the caller's executable.

## Promotion gates

Promote the contract/safety portion only if the exact candidate:

- preserves all 152 completion, zero-future-use, input immutability, and
  primary-immutability results;
- reduces canonical direction/estimate inconsistencies from 33 to zero;
- makes all 24 insufficient-cycle answers typed abstentions with null numeric
  claims and an explicit identifiability reason;
- eliminates zero-width intervals from weak rows that have calibration errors;
- retains zero automation-eligible unadmitted-season and insufficient-cycle
  rows;
- never upgrades support relative to baseline and keeps the existing minimum
  fold requirement;
- preserves non-seasonal trend accuracy within 2 percentage points by family;
- produces non-negative matched selective-utility change overall;
- exposes unmodelled seasonal evidence without silently changing the declared
  period; and
- passes focused and complete isolated tests.

The numeric mechanism graduates beyond a safety subfix only if the parent Q2
accuracy, coverage, matched utility, and no-regression gates pass. Otherwise
retain its evidence as a no-build or revise it without weakening support.
