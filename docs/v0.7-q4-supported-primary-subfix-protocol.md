# v0.7 Q4 supported-primary replay stability subfix

Status: frozen after both development baselines and before production change.

## Observed boundary

Both same-input development replications completed 6/6 and selected the same
normalized role, method, and scenario ID on every task. Each improved mean and
median official RCRPS, won five rows, lost one, and had a positive 90% paired
bootstrap lower bound. Both nevertheless failed the frozen task-harm gate.

The one harmful recommendation was an observation counterfactual that
automatically displaced a fully supported immutable primary. Its prefix-only
replay cleared point and probabilistic margins but won exactly two of three
chronological blocks—the current minimum. The successful periodic observation
counterfactual won all three blocks. The realized task score identifies the
row for diagnosis only and may not enter the mechanism.

## Frozen candidate

Expose the number of chronological replay blocks evaluated. When all of the
following hold:

- the immutable primary is `supported`;
- an observation counterfactual is otherwise replay-admitted;
- all three chronological blocks were evaluated; and
- the counterfactual does not beat the strongest probabilistic comparator in
  every block,

withhold that path from human recommendation selection. Preserve the path,
its `conditionally_supported` replay status, point/probabilistic measurements,
claims, seal, and immutable primary. Add a typed recommendation-stability
reason explaining that admission evidence was not uniform enough to displace
a fully supported primary. Automation remains false.

Do not change replay fitting, comparator families, numeric margins, support,
strict publication, primary numerics, or behavior when the primary is degraded
or conditional. Missing legacy block-count metadata retains prior behavior;
the current fitter always emits the count. No task identity, seed, future,
RCRPS, selected role, or benchmark label is a runtime input.

## Subfix gates

- A supported primary remains selected for a 2/3-block replay candidate, while
  the candidate and typed stability reason remain visible.
- A 3/3-block replay candidate retains its existing deterministic Evidence
  recommendation.
- A 2/3-block replay remains eligible when the primary is not fully supported.
- Existing replay, publication, seal, selection-contract, immutable-primary,
  and automation tests pass.
- Rerun the identical two development replications in new candidate
  directories. The parent Q4 gates decide whether untouched confirmation may
  run; do not revise this rule from their future scores.
