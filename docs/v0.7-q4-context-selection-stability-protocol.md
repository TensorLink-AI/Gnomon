# v0.7 Q4 stable context-mechanism selection

Status: frozen before selection-only harness work, fresh execution, or
production change.

## User boundary and prior evidence

Agents should receive the same recommendation mechanism when the same
pre-cutoff evidence is replayed, and humans should see why that mechanism won.
A favorable mean driven by one hindsight outlier is insufficient.

The old four-task sensor comparison measured Gnomon's complete Evidence
pipeline against a separate direct-agent control: mean RCRPS improved, median
worsened, and Gnomon lost three of four rows. Post-outcome diagnostics also
show that Gnomon's selected publication beat its own immutable primary on each
row where it changed mechanisms. The former result is weak generalization
evidence; the latter is selector evidence. Q4 does not conflate them.

Use these six exact official CiK task classes, covering observation repair,
calibration repair, isolated events, implicit maintenance, dated distractors,

- `SensorPeriodicMaintenanceTask`;

- `SensorSpikeTask`;
- `ATMUnderPeriodicMaintenanceTaskWithConclusionLessExplicit`;
- `ElectricityIncreaseInPredictionWithDistractorWithDates`; and
- `UnemploymentCountyUsingSingleStateData`.

Development uses previously unused task seed `7041`. Run two independent
no-cache replications, `a` and `b`, without changing input, prompt, model, or
configuration. Untouched confirmation uses task seed `7042`, again with two
independent replications, only after one exact mechanism clears development.
Task names and seeds select work only; task identity, seed, future values,
scores, and selected labels may not enter production behavior.

Use the Evidence profile, DeepSeek `deepseek-v4-flash-0731` through Engy,
`publication_best_effort`, temperature zero, reasoning effort none for
governed structured calls, 50 submitted samples, no benchmark cache,
`max-parallel=1`, a 900-second per-case timeout, 4 GiB per-case memory ceiling,
2 GiB free-memory stop, atomic case checkpoints, and default bounded model/tool
deadlines. Resume completed rows; missing and failed rows remain in all
denominators. A selection-only repeatable exact-task CLI may be added to the
benchmark harness before execution, with parser/selection tests, but it may
not alter tasks, forecasts, publication, scoring, or product code.

## Frozen measures

For every task and replication retain:

- selected scenario ID, normalized role, selection method, selection-pass
  disposition, support, recommendation authority, and automation eligibility;
- complete eligible portfolio and the pre-cutoff evidence fields presented to
  selection;
- selected and immutable-primary official RCRPS, outcome-scored candidate
  shadows, selected-versus-primary gain, and hindsight regret reported only
  after forecasting;
- selected path seal, compiler/selector/model calls, tokens, retries, latency,
  completion, and resume state; and
- canonical-primary identity and temporal leakage.

Same-input stability compares replications `a` and `b` without looking at
future scores: normalized role and selection method must agree; a primary/prior
flip is always material. Report path-seal agreement separately because a
stable mechanism can still carry a stochastic prior distribution. Official
proper scores are used only after selection is frozen.

Matched improvement is `primary RCRPS - selected RCRPS`, so positive is
better. Report mean, median, wins, losses, ties, a deterministic paired
bootstrap interval, and each task independently. A selected primary is an
honest zero-gain tie, not a dropped row.

## Development gate

Proceed to confirmation or a production candidate only if both development
replications have:

- 6/6 completion, zero leakage, exact immutable-primary identity, and complete
  selection/authority/accounting contracts;
- identical normalized role and selection method on at least five of six
  same-input pairs, with no unexplained primary/prior flip;
- lower mean and median selected RCRPS than immutable-primary RCRPS;
- more wins than losses, counting all six rows and retaining ties;
- a non-negative deterministic 90% paired-bootstrap lower bound on mean gain;
- no task whose selected RCRPS exceeds its primary by more than 2%; and
- automation false for every prior-assisted or human-review recommendation.

If the current selector clears this gate, treat Q4 as a validated no-build and
run untouched confirmation. If it fails, inspect every unstable or harmful
row by the pre-cutoff evidence actually supplied. Freeze one general candidate
mechanism before modifying production behavior.

## Candidate and confirmation gate

A candidate may use only pre-cutoff evidence strength, replay quality,
support, provenance, and path-stability facts already present or generally
derivable. It may not use task identity, seed, selected benchmark role,
realized future, RCRPS, or hindsight candidate ranking. It must preserve every
candidate and the immutable primary even when it changes the recommendation.

Promote—or close the current selector as a no-build—only if development and
untouched confirmation both satisfy the development gates, selected mean and
median RCRPS are lower than primary, wins exceed losses, the 90% paired lower
bound is non-negative, no task regresses beyond 2%, and same-input normalized
selection stability is at least five of six. The exact candidate must also
pass focused publication/agent tests, a small actual-response inspection, and
the full TSFM-isolated suite. Otherwise remove or disable the candidate and
retain an evidence-backed no-build or unresolved checkpoint.
