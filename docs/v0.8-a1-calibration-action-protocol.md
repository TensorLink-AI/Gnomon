# v0.8 A1 calibration and action-tier protocol

Status: frozen before A1 benchmark construction or implementation.

## User boundary

Agents and humans should see whether uncertainty is calibrated for this exact
series/model/horizon and what kind of action, if any, that evidence may
authorize. Missing, stale, pooled-after-selection, mismatched, or
insufficient calibration fails closed. A probability or interval cannot gain
authority from confident prose.

Action tiers are `advisory`, `reversible_low_impact`, and `high_impact`.
Advisory results may inform a decision but never actuate. v0.8 may authorize a
host-controlled reversible low-impact action only when the selected path and
its exact calibration lineage clear all gates. High-impact automation always
remains false.

## Frozen development matrix

Construct twelve label-free publication cases after this protocol is
committed. Cross:

- action tier: omitted legacy, advisory, reversible low impact, high impact;
- support: supported, context-trusted, weak, and unsupported;
- calibration: absent, exact matched, wrong series, wrong model, wrong
  horizon, pooled across selection, out-of-band coverage, too few points, and
  stale/unknown cutoff;
- caller channel: trusted host or advisory MCP; and
- policy: complete, incomplete, unauthorized, and legacy `allow` alias.

The baseline and candidate receive identical result dictionaries and policy
objects. No benchmark IDs, expected actions, or hidden futures enter product
behavior.

Calibration development seed is `2026083701`; untouched confirmation seed is
`2026083702`. Deterministic forecast evaluation spans at least 200 cases across
level, trend, seasonal, intermittent, heteroskedastic, and regime-shift
families, horizons 3 and 12, nominal 80% intervals, both pooled and strict
split calibration, and multiple frequencies. Report empirical coverage,
interval score/width, completion, lineage completeness, and family slices.

## Calibration lineage contract

The publication receives only calibration evidence already sealed into the
same forecast artifact's `rolling_evaluation` record. It must bind:

- artifact ID and series;
- selected executable/model;
- requested horizon and forecast row count;
- nominal interval level and emitted quantiles;
- residual-fold count and whether selection folds were pooled;
- measured held-out coverage, point denominator, and cutoff/as-of status; and
- an exact eligibility verdict with typed failed checks.

Adaptive coverage outcomes remain point-in-time replayable diagnostics. They
must not alter a published interval or authorize an action until project,
series, executable, horizon, frequency, and outcome-knowledge cohort are all
explicitly matched and prospective validation passes.

## Promotion gates

The local policy matrix requires exact lineage and tier projection, zero
authorization for advisory/high-impact/untrusted/incomplete/mismatched/
uncalibrated cases, no interval mutation, no primary mutation, exact legacy
behavior when the new tier is omitted, deterministic seals, and executable
repair reasons.

Reversible low-impact graduation additionally requires on development and
untouched confirmation:

- exact artifact/series/model/horizon lineage and no future leakage;
- strict split calibration rather than residuals pooled across model-selection
  folds;
- at least ten held-out points and an empirical 80% interval coverage point
  estimate in `[0.65, 0.95]`;
- coverage within 10 percentage points of nominal on untouched aggregate data
  with no family miss larger than 15 points;
- non-worsening interval score, proper score, and false-action cost versus the
  frozen baseline;
- positive selective utility at the proposed coverage;
- no action-tier, support, probability, or automation inflation; and
- focused tests plus the full suite isolated from machine-local TSFM supply.

If no exact candidate clears these gates, ship the typed lineage/tier
disclosure but retain reversible automation as false. That is a useful product
boundary, not evidence to claim calibrated automation.
