# v0.8 F1 StatsForecast decision

Decision: ship the adapter as explicit opt-in; do not enable it by default and
do not claim forecast-quality improvement.

## What changed for users

`gnomon-forecast[statsforecast]` installs a lazy adapter for StatsForecast
2.1.x. A user may explicitly set `models.statsforecast.enabled=true`; the
adapter then enters Gnomon's existing prefix-only contest against last-value,
seasonal-naive, historical mean, and the built-ins. Missing/incompatible
packages, excessive outer-fold cost, excessive seasonal periods, partial fold
completion, and backend numerical warnings are disclosed as structured soft
skips or receipts. They never disable the controls or silently substitute a
path.

The four preregistered components are selected inside one
`statsforecast_auto` representative on a trailing validation slice of each
visible prefix. Only that representative enters the outer contest. Its final
fit uses at most the same disclosed 256-point visible suffix. This removed the
multiple-candidate tournament side effect observed in the first development
implementation.

The dependency-free base, default candidate pool, immutable request, Gnomon
residual intervals, support boundary, and automation policy are unchanged.

## Evidence and decision

The first four-outer-candidate development implementation completed 200/200
cases and passed the absolute legacy selector gates, but only one plugin path
published. Two rows changed versus control: the plugin displaced a useful
built-in on one, and the enlarged tournament changed a confirmation fallback
on another. Aggregate metrics were unchanged. That implementation was
revised once as permitted by the frozen protocol.

The single-representative revision produced exact control parity on the
frozen 200-case development screen:

- 200/200 complete, zero future observations used, no silent fallback;
- 92 departures, 69 wins and 23 losses, 75% departure precision;
- median departure gain 0.5162, deterministic 90% interval
  `[0.3195, 0.6471]`;
- zero plugin publications and zero changed final paths versus control;
- median/p95/max measured evaluation latency
  0.0012/0.0662/1.0972 seconds.

Untouched seed `2026083302` repeated exact control parity on 100/100 cases:
45 departures, 36 wins and 9 losses, 80% precision, median departure gain
0.6241, 90% interval `[0.3094, 0.7960]`, and zero plugin publications.

The first alternate-cutoff naturalistic shard completed eight cases. The
bounded portfolio soft-skipped excessive-fold cases and published twice on
retail: one +0.8% held-out gain and one -2.5% loss. Those product evaluations
took 86.27 and 60.74 seconds; shard p95 was 60.74 seconds, above the frozen
30-second gate. A preceding unbounded diagnostic hit its 300-second wall-clock
limit. The remaining naturalistic shards were not run after the frozen latency
kill criterion had become impossible to pass.

Therefore F1 does not address the observed frequency of last-value-like
forecasts in this release. The optional adapter is retained because its
conformance, provenance, warning capture, missing-dependency behavior,
compute-budget refusal, baseline safety, and executable identity are useful to
operators running explicit experiments. `statsforecast_enabled` remains false
by default. Default promotion requires a future prospective protocol with a
batch or precomputed fold path, at least ten independently useful naturalistic
publications, and the same accuracy/latency gates; this result may not be
relabelled as that evidence.
