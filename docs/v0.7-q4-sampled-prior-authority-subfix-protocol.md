# v0.7 Q4 sampled-prior recommendation-authority subfix

Status: frozen after the first Q4 confirmation attempt and before the second
production change.

## Observed boundary

The first supported-primary replay fix cleared both development replications.
The untouched seed-7042 attempt then exposed a different authority defect. On
`SensorSpikeTask`, a host-sampled model prior displaced a `supported` immutable
primary through `best_effort_sampled_prior_policy` and scored 28.9% worse. The
same policy also changed the selected electricity mechanism between identical
replications. In both rows the publication truthfully reported
`historical_skill_evidence: false`; sampled-path agreement was the only added
authority.

This is distinct from the useful unemployment selection. That task had a
`best_effort` primary and used a separate governed selection pass with cited
evidence and counterevidence. Seed 7042 identifies the general defect but is
now revision evidence, not untouched confirmation for the revised mechanism.

## Frozen candidate

When all of the following hold:

- the immutable primary support is `supported` or `context_trusted`;
- a candidate role is `model_authored`;
- the candidate is a host-observed sampled-point-path prior; and
- its elicitation explicitly has no historical skill evidence,

preserve the sealed prior and its distribution, claims, diagnostics, and
outcome-scoring identity, but withhold it from both deterministic and governed
human recommendation selection. Add a typed recommendation-stability reason
stating that sampled agreement is not sufficient authority to displace the
fully supported primary. Automation remains false.

This boundary does not suppress deterministic effects, governed mappings,
historically replayed candidates, outcome-graduated priors, transformations,
or model priors when the primary itself is degraded or best-effort. It does
not change numerics, support, fitting, elicitation, primary forecasts, or
strict publication. No task identity, seed, future value, score, selected
role, or benchmark label is a runtime input.

## Subfix and revised-confirmation gates

- A sufficient sampled prior remains visible but cannot displace a supported
  or context-trusted primary, including through an explicit selection payload.
- The same prior remains human-selectable when the primary is `best_effort`.
- An outcome-graduated prior and deterministic/context-replay paths retain
  their existing authority.
- Existing publication, selection-contract, candidate-preservation, seal,
  immutable-primary, automation, and benchmark safety tests pass.
- Treat both seed-7042 replications as revision diagnostics. Rerun them only to
  verify that the observed authority failure is removed; do not call them
  confirmation for this revised candidate.
- If the revised candidate clears the parent Q4 gates on the revision set, run
  two untouched no-cache replications at previously unused seed `7043` with
  the exact six tasks and frozen Q4 configuration. Those two replications are
  the revised candidate's independent confirmation. The parent Q4 gates apply
  unchanged.
