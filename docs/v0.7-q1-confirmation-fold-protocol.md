# v0.7 Q1 independent confirmation-fold protocol

Status: frozen before production implementation.

## Diagnosed boundary

The 48-case naturalistic confirmation completed cleanly but the production
selector's departures produced 15 wins, 15 losses, and 6 ties against the
strongest honest reference. Selection already requires mean improvement,
majority fold wins, positive median relative gain, and fold-local scaled-error
improvement. Those checks all reuse the same selection origins.

Production also reserves calibration and final-test origins, but neither can
currently prevent a selection-fold winner from publishing. Reusing the final
test as a veto would make its reported score selection-contaminated. The
general boundary defect is therefore a missing independent confirmation
stage, not a need for dataset-specific routing or a hindsight model map.

## Frozen candidate

For an ordinary forecast with at least five disjoint origins, partition in
chronological order:

1. selection origins;
2. one confirmation origin;
3. one interval-calibration origin; and
4. one final test origin.

Selection runs unchanged on its earlier origins. If it chooses a non-baseline
path, that one already-chosen contender is compared with the already-chosen
strongest baseline on the confirmation origin using the same WAPE metric. The
contender proceeds only when its confirmation error is no greater than the
baseline error. This is a non-inferiority veto, not a second tournament: no
other candidate is considered, and no minimum uplift is estimated from one
fold. Failure or an uncomputable comparison publishes the strongest baseline.

The confirmation origin is prefix-visible at forecast time and unavailable to
the earlier selection contest. The calibration and final-test origins remain
unread during confirmation. Confirmation outcome, scores, origin role, and
fallback are exposed under selection-stability evidence.

Runs with fewer than five origins preserve their existing degraded behavior;
manufacturing a confirmation fold by overlapping would overstate evidence.
Threshold jobs preserve their existing nine-origin event-calibration/test
partition in this iteration; changing that separately frozen decision surface
would confound the candidate.

## Evaluation

The candidate is tested on:

- the unchanged 48-case naturalistic corpus frozen in
  `docs/v0.7-q1-naturalistic-confirmation-protocol.md`;
- the unchanged 200-case synthetic selector screen, seed 92741;
- short-history, threshold-partition, candidate identity, interval
  provenance, support, multi-target, and golden-artifact tests.

The subfix is promoted only if:

- every product case and provenance gate stays complete;
- the naturalistic candidate has more departure wins than losses and every
  group median remains within -2%;
- harmful naturalistic departures decrease without fewer total wins than
  losses;
- all frozen synthetic gates continue to pass with no family median
  regression beyond 2%;
- final-test and calibration origins remain disjoint from both selection and
  confirmation; and
- actual forecast evidence names confirmation fallback without upgrading
  support or claiming that the contender won the final test.

This subfix does not by itself complete Q1. The stricter original naturalistic
gate (including 70% positive-gain precision and clustered uncertainty) remains
reported exactly as preregistered; a safer but still unconfirmed policy stays
open for further investigation or a retained no-build decision.
