Decision: the accumulated v0.8 work is safe enough for draft review, but a
v0.8 release recommendation and broad forecast- or agent-skill claims are
withheld.

## What is ready

The production candidate is `b512365`. It preserves the immutable primary
forecast, exact typed relationships, support and automation boundaries while
adding general agent-facing contracts, an optional governed StatsForecast
adapter, calibration lineage, action tiers, and crash-safe evaluation paths.
The full isolated repository suite passed with 2,692 tests and 12 skips. A
wheel and source distribution built successfully; a clean wheel install
completed the forecast, publication verification, decision-seal, calibration
lineage, protected-automation, and MCP discovery smoke checks.

The numeric property, selector, and naturalistic shards were executed at
`e11ee61`. Their inspection exposed three general boundary defects: duplicate
compact reasoning content, an oversized tool schema, and a repair prompt that
omitted the valid option vocabulary. Commit `b512365` fixes only those
presentation and repair boundaries; it does not change numeric forecast
selection. The affected 22-test boundary suite and full repository suite were
rerun at `b512365`, and the ContextBench diagnostic ran after that fix.

The final local property run completed 312 primary cases plus its registered
stress suites and passed every frozen safety and property gate. The 200-case
production selector run also passed its frozen synthetic gates: 91 departures,
71 wins, 20 losses, 78.02% departure precision, and a 54.60% median relative
gain among departures with a 90% interval of [43.00%, 69.94%]. These results
support contract integrity and controlled synthetic usefulness; they are not
a claim of broad real-world superiority.

The exact-head 112-case ContextBench engine confirmation completed at one
worker with zero leakage, exact disposition and typed-scenario contracts,
publication parity, and primary-forecast parity. All 16 structural cases kept
the canonical primary unchanged. Two exposed a typed conditional scenario;
the other 14 retained `no_distinct_numeric_path`. This is engine-contract
evidence only. It does not show that a hosted agent preserved or used those
relationships in its final answer.

## What remains bounded

The 120-case, digest-pinned naturalistic selector confirmation passed every
completion, leakage, provenance, reference-score, and fallback safety gate,
but failed the frozen quality gates. Across 77 departures it recorded 36 wins,
23 losses, and 18 ties; departure precision was 46.75%, median departure gain
was zero, and six departures were invalidated by the independently scored
historical mean. StatsForecast therefore remains explicit opt-in and disabled
by default. No broad forecast-quality claim is warranted.

Context admission also remains deliberately conservative. The reused
112-case diagnostic seed produced 100% observed empirical admission precision
but only 6.94% recall, and therefore was not decision-ready under its fresh-seed
and high-recall gates. C1's current strict admission boundary remains in place.

Ordinary artifacts do not carry passed prospective calibration evidence, so
reversible automation remains disabled; high-impact automation is always
disabled. The additive decision and action contracts improve the handoff and
make abstention reasons inspectable, but hosted decision utility is not yet
established.

## External evidence gap

No Engy or other hosted-provider credential is available in this environment.
Consequently the frozen minimum of 24 fresh ContextBench/TemporalBench agent
rows and 12 fresh CiK cases was not run. Deterministic engine tests do not
substitute for final-agent preservation, decision utility, request/token
economy, or external benchmark performance. The pull request must remain a
draft while these lanes are pending.

The release, tag, merge, and publication decisions remain separate operations.
