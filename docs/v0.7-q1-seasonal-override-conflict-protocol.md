# v0.7 Q1 seasonal override-conflict subfix protocol

Status: frozen after the exact baseline and an exploratory dirty-tree probe,
before promotion, exact candidate scoring, or release.

## Baseline diagnosis

SeasonalBench completed all 128 cases at exact commit `289c12d`. Automatic
detection found the planted period 6 in all sufficient stable-season cases,
and every fold-starved non-baseline publication was caveated with its fold
count. The dangerous behavior was narrower: an explicit neighboring-period
override could contradict strong visible autocorrelation while the result
still appeared fully supported. The caller's override changed the mandatory
seasonal baseline and candidate fits, but the conflict itself was absent from
agent/human evidence.

Eight held-out harmful departures were labelled supported. Seven occurred
where an explicit period 5 or 7 contradicted a visible autocorrelation period;
the eighth was a mandatory historical-mean baseline with zero uplift claim on
a no-season sample. Mandatory baselines are safe retention, not incremental
model admissions; the frozen parent protocol explicitly permits a
“baseline/caveat” in ambiguous families.

The parent protocol also showed that wrong-period numeric output can be far
worse than the paired correct-period output. Silently replacing a caller's
explicit period with the benchmark's planted period would violate caller
authority and optimize to generator labels. This subfix therefore addresses
the general disclosure/authority defect only; the original numeric
neighbor-period gate remains reported as failed.

## Frozen candidate

When and only when:

- the caller supplied an explicit seasonal-period override;
- the visible history independently selected a different autocorrelation
  period under the existing detector threshold; and
- the detector already satisfied its two-visible-cycle requirement,

honor the caller's period and leave every model score and forecast number
unchanged, but:

- add typed reason `seasonal_period_override_conflict` with requested period,
  detected period, detector basis, and strength;
- cap a supported assessment at `conditionally_supported`;
- add recovery `review_seasonal_period_override`, naming both the domain-truth
  and automatic-detection options; and
- expose used/detected period evidence and `override_conflict` in temporal
  facts.

Matching overrides, automatic detection, frequency defaults, and histories
without a qualifying autocorrelation detection remain unchanged. The
candidate never substitutes a period or routes on family, seed, or future
outcome.

## Promotion gates

Promote this safety subfix only if:

- all 128 SeasonalBench product cases still complete with zero future use;
- all qualifying explicit conflicts are conditionally supported and carry
  the typed reason, recovery, and temporal facts;
- supported harmful non-baseline departures under an override conflict fall
  to zero;
- matching and automatic period cases preserve model identity, scores,
  forecast losses, and support absent unrelated calibration warnings;
- caller overrides remain the period actually used;
- the original parent gates and numeric paired losses are reported unchanged,
  including failures rather than relabelled;
- focused runtime, frequency, selection, support, tool, and golden tests pass;
  and
- the complete isolated suite passes.

Passing these gates promotes only honest authority and recovery UX. It does
not establish that wrong periods are numerically robust, nor that a selected
model beats the strongest hindsight seasonal reference.
