# V0.6 P5 outcome-adaptive routing protocol

Status: frozen before production changes on 2026-08-30.

## User hypothesis

An agent should be able to reuse a pinned challenger which has repeatedly
beaten its champion on paired realised outcomes, without silently promoting it
or carrying stale success through a regime change. A human should see the
candidate, revision, champion, cohort, cutoff, evidence count, improvement,
uncertainty, recent behavior, decision, and exact rollback condition.

The recommendation narrows a future candidate contest only. Every forecast
still performs its job-local admission against mandatory baselines, so tracking
memory can never publish a number or confer automation authority by itself.

## Audit finding and bounded change

`gnomon_route` currently consults an observational selected-model leaderboard.
That evidence is not paired, has no route-level `as_of`, applies its sample
floor across the whole project instead of per candidate, and does not reset on
recent degradation. It therefore remains descriptive/advisory and will not be
given adaptive authority in this iteration.

`AdapterOutcomeLedger` already stores paired challenger/champion errors with a
knowledge time, pinned revision, and optional temporal regime. Its assessment
is the smallest safe seam. The treatment may add a distinct routing assessment
which:

1. requires an explicit timezone-aware `as_of` and normalises all knowledge
   times to UTC before storage and comparison;
2. matches project, candidate, pinned revision, champion, and exact declared
   regime;
3. requires at least eight paired outcomes, at least 5% mean relative gain,
   and a 95% Wilson win-rate lower bound above 0.5;
4. applies a four-outcome recent window and rolls back to the champion when its
   mean gain is non-positive or its win rate falls below 0.5;
5. returns a candidate-pool recommendation, evidence receipt, and rollback
   condition while keeping `automatic_promotion=false`,
   `automation_eligible=false`, and `job_local_admission_required=true`.

Existing shadow promotion assessment stays backward compatible. No routing
decision mutates stored outcomes, configuration, or a forecast artifact.

## Frozen prospective streams

The local benchmark replays four deterministic paired-error streams in strict
knowledge-time order. At each step, routing is assessed using only preceding
outcomes; the next pair scores the recommendation.

- `stable_gain`: 20 outcomes, challenger error 0.70 and champion error 1.00.
- `gain_then_drift`: 12 outcomes at 0.70/1.00 followed by eight at 1.40/1.00.
- `mixed_control`: 20 alternating outcomes at 0.90/1.00 and 1.10/1.00, which
  has no stable advantage.
- `regime_isolation`: eight beneficial `subdaily` outcomes plus eight harmful
  `daily_weekly` outcomes for the same candidate; exact-regime routes must not
  pool them.

The candidate is `challenger@sha256:route-v1`; the champion is `last_value`.
The first outcome is known at `2026-01-01T00:00:00Z`, with one-day increments.
A separate future-knowledge probe inserts an apparently excellent outcome
after a replay cutoff and requires byte-equivalent routing before that cutoff.
Stream names and next errors are benchmark-only and are not inputs to routing.

## Frozen gates

All missing or failed decisions remain in denominators. Promotion requires:

- **Completion:** every prospective decision and outcome completes serially.
- **Cold start:** the first eight decisions in each fresh cohort recommend the
  champion with `insufficient_paired_outcomes`; no one-off win routes.
- **Stable skill:** `stable_gain` routes the challenger once eligible and its
  cumulative routed error improves at least 15% over always-champion.
- **Drift rollback:** `gain_then_drift` routes the challenger during the stable
  segment, returns to the champion within at most two harmful observations,
  and has non-positive regret versus always-champion over the complete stream.
- **Control safety:** `mixed_control` never routes the challenger and is exactly
  equal to always-champion.
- **Regime isolation:** the subdaily cohort can route the challenger while the
  daily/weekly cohort routes the champion; neither cohort's counts include the
  other.
- **Point-in-time replay:** adding an outcome with `known_at > as_of` changes no
  field of the earlier routing decision, and equivalent offsets normalise to
  the same UTC ordering.
- **Revision safety:** an unpinned candidate never routes.
- **Authority:** every response states no automatic promotion, no automation
  eligibility, job-local admission required, the recommended pool, and a
  rollback condition naming the champion.
- **Determinism:** identical ledger plus `as_of` produces an identical decision
  and receipt ID.
- **Compatibility:** existing `assess_adapter_shadow` payloads and tests remain
  unchanged apart from additive fields only if unavoidable.

## Execution and inspection

The benchmark is local, serial, and writes one atomic checkpoint per stream.
It uses one retry and a 30-second stream timeout. The retained record includes
the evaluated commit, dirty-tree state, stream definition version, every
point-in-time recommendation, routed and champion errors, rollback delay,
denominators, and hashes.

Actual responses are inspected at cold start, stable graduation, first drift,
rollback, mixed control, and both isolated regimes. Engine contract completeness
is recorded separately from any later agent phrasing; no API agent probe is
needed unless the compact tool host drops or contradicts routing authority.

## Kill and no-build rules

Reject or revise if routing reads future outcomes, pools regimes implicitly,
routes an unpinned revision, treats observational leaderboard rows as paired
evidence, weakens job-local admission, or cannot roll back within the frozen
delay. Do not tune sample, gain, Wilson, or recent-window thresholds after
observing the frozen streams. If the safe paired route cannot beat the champion
without violating a safety gate, retain assessment-only behavior and record a
no-build decision.
