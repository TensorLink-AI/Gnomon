# v0.8 F1 governed StatsForecast candidate protocol

Status: frozen before production implementation or candidate scoring.

## User boundary

Gnomon often retains an assumption-light baseline. That is the right answer
when visible-prefix evidence is weak, but it leaves agents without a useful
non-flat path when a well-established statistical family could forecast the
series better. Installing a model library must not silently turn availability
into authority.

This iteration tests an optional StatsForecast adapter. When the extra is
installed, a small preregistered family enters Gnomon's existing prefix-only
rolling-origin contest alongside last-value, seasonal-naive, historical mean,
and the built-in candidates. The same selection margin, independent
confirmation veto, residual calibration, final-test partition, executable
candidate contract, and fallback policy apply. Dataset identity, family label,
case ID, and held-out values are unavailable to production selection.

Humans and agents must be able to see the package version, exact model,
seasonal period, completed/failed folds, score, admission decision, and any
soft skip. Native StatsForecast intervals are not published in this
iteration; Gnomon's fold-separated residual calibration remains authoritative.

## Frozen candidate family

- `statsforecast_autoets`: `AutoETS(season_length=season)`;
- `statsforecast_autoarima`: `AutoARIMA(season_length=season)`;
- `statsforecast_autotheta`: `AutoTheta(season_length=season)`;
- `statsforecast_croston_optimized`: `CrostonOptimized()` for non-negative
  intermittent demand only.

Each automatic model is one internally selected family representative and
therefore one outer candidate. MSTL is excluded because this corpus does not
independently represent enough histories with multiple complete seasonalities.
The supported package line is `statsforecast>=2.1,<3`; the evaluated revision
is recorded at runtime. The base Gnomon installation remains dependency-free.
Missing, incompatible, or failing optional dependencies produce an explicit
soft skip and never suppress the mandatory baselines.

## Frozen development and confirmation

The development screen uses the existing production-selector generator with
fresh seed `2026083301`, 40 cases per family, and the five generator-owned
families `level`, `trend`, `seasonal`, `pseudo_seasonal`, and `intermittent`.
This yields 200 cases across horizon 3 and fold-starved horizon 12 lanes.
Candidate code receives only each numeric prefix, horizon, declared period,
and cadence. The final horizon remains sealed until scoring.

One implementation revision is allowed after development. If development is
green, untouched synthetic confirmation uses seed `2026083302`, 20 cases per
family (100 cases). Naturalistic confirmation then uses the 12 vendored public
series and alternate cutoffs `0.55`, `0.65`, `0.75`, and `0.85`, frozen before
implementation. Dataset paths and SHA-256 identities remain those declared in
`benchmarks/modelbench/run_naturalistic_selector.py`; short and long horizons
alternate exactly as in that harness. These 48 cases are not pooled with the
synthetic screen.

The control arm disables StatsForecast discovery explicitly. The treatment
arm enables exactly the four registered candidates. Machine-local TSFM
discovery is disabled in both arms. Runs are sequential and resumable; each
row records completion and a failed row remains in the denominator.

## Frozen measurements and promotion gates

Held-out MAE is measured for the published path and for last-value,
seasonal-naive when eligible, and historical mean. A departure differs
numerically from last-value. Its bounded relative gain is
`(reference_mae - candidate_mae) / max(reference_mae, candidate_mae, 1e-12)`,
where the reference is the strongest of those simple controls and is chosen
only after forecasting.

Development and untouched synthetic confirmation each require:

- at least 99% product completion, zero future leakage, and no silent fallback;
- at least 20 admitted departures;
- at least 70% positive-gain precision among departures;
- positive median bounded relative gain, more wins than losses, and a
  non-negative deterministic 90% paired-bootstrap lower bound;
- no preregistered family median all-case regression beyond 2%;
- complete package/model/fold/score/failure/selection provenance;
- exact mandatory-baseline and immutable-request preservation; and
- deterministic replay for every adapter that completes.

Naturalistic treatment must be non-inferior to control on completion, future
isolation, fallback disclosure, departure precision, wins minus losses,
median departure gain, cluster-bootstrap lower bound, group median all-case
gain, and mean-invalidated departures. It must not turn a control win into an
unsupported harmful departure. At least one StatsForecast model must be the
published executable on ten naturalistic cases before installed-by-default
discovery can be promoted.

Runtime overhead is reported separately. The small-family treatment must keep
median evaluation latency under ten seconds and p95 under thirty seconds on
the frozen synthetic screen; exceeding either is a no-default decision even
if accuracy gates pass. Individual model failures are bounded by the caller's
evaluation and never retried invisibly.

If any strict gate fails, the adapter may still ship as explicit opt-in when
its conformance, provenance, soft-skip, and safety gates pass, but it may not
be enabled merely because the dependency is installed. A failed model or
family is not replaced with another unregistered family in this iteration.
