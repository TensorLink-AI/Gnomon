# P2 protocol: useful short-history forecasts without hidden certainty

Status: preregistered before the I002 implementation or fresh candidate run.

## User hypothesis

When a series is too short for Gnomon's ordinary separated evaluation folds,
an independently validated pretrained candidate can give an agent a more
useful path than last-value forecasting. A human should receive that added
signal only when its transfer evidence and a local prequential check justify
it; otherwise the existing robust fallback, support tier, and warning must
remain unchanged.

This iteration asks two questions separately:

1. **Supply:** can the pinned candidate itself beat the robust engine baseline
   on previously unseen, naturally sourced short histories?
2. **Governance:** can Gnomon's evidence-weighted admission policy capture
   useful candidate signal without turning an unsupported prior into an
   apparently validated forecast?

An agent-facing benchmark is not needed to answer either numeric question.
Agent preservation is tested later under P8 after the engine contract is
settled.

## Frozen candidate and evidence

- Candidate: `toto2_4m`, resolved by the sandbox to the pinned revision named
  in every output. A changed or missing revision is a different experiment.
- Independent transfer registry:
  `results/toto2-transfer-registry-20260820/model-evidence.json`.
- The registry contains 84 held-out synthetic cases and no TemporalBench
  cases. Its synthetic provenance must remain visible; it is permission to
  test transfer, not proof of real-world superiority.
- Runtime selection may use history, horizon, frequency, local prequential
  evidence, and the versioned registry. It may not use source-dataset names,
  task IDs, benchmark labels, answer choices, or held-out futures.

## Matched arms

Every arm uses the identical TemporalBench T2 rows and official future values
only for post-hoc scoring.

| Arm | Purpose | Configuration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B | Robust baseline | `gnomon-pure --best-effort --forecast-candidates last_value,seasonal_naive,drift,linear_trend,window_average,theta,ets,croston_sba`; the frozen classical pool cannot change when an optional sandbox is installed |
| S | Candidate supply | `gnomon-pure --named-tsfm toto2_4m`; explicitly experimental and not governed |
| G | Governed admission | `gnomon-pure --model-evidence-registry ...`; current evidence-weighted policy |

Only one instance of each underlying forecasting case is used: T2 is selected
instead of the duplicate T4 forecast view. Runs are sequential
(`forecast_jobs=1`), resumable, and retained with manifests and row details.
Local inference receives a 20-minute wall-clock bound per smoke arm. There are
no provider retries because no API is involved. A failed channel stays in the
denominator.

## Corpus and staged denominators

The fresh naturalistic screen uses the retained, labeled TemporalBench split.
The short-history domains are:

- MIMIC: 50-observation clinical histories with multiple target channels.
- causal_chambers: the shortest available 118--300 observation physical-system
  histories. Expansion reports the history-length mix rather than silently
  treating all records as equally short.

Smoke uses the first two deterministic T2 cases from each named domain. This
is an infrastructure and gross-harm screen, not a promotion result. If it
passes, expansion uses the first 10 deterministic T2 cases from each domain.
Any later full run must freeze its larger denominator in a checkpoint before
starting and may not replace the matched 20-case result.

Scores are paired per forecast channel after joining by task and channel.
Reported denominators include selected cases, expected channels, completed
channels, abstentions, failures, and scoreable pairs. Headline error is sMAPE;
MASE is secondary because constant or nearly constant short histories can make
its scale unstable. Results report the paired median relative sMAPE change,
win/loss/tie counts, a paired bootstrap interval, and the same measures by
named domain.

## Frozen gates

### Smoke gate

- all four selected rows terminate in every arm;
- every successful S forecast exposes candidate name, backend, pinned
  revision, and experimental selection policy;
- no non-finite forecast values, horizon mismatch, temporal leakage, silent
  fallback, or missing support label;
- S is not worse than B by more than 10% in paired median sMAPE overall.

Failure of an identity or safety invariant kills the candidate until fixed.
A gross-error failure rejects this candidate family for P2 without tuning to
the failed case.

### Matched expansion gate

Candidate supply S must satisfy all of:

- at least 99% channel completion and no silent fallback;
- at least 5% paired median relative sMAPE improvement over B overall;
- more channel wins than losses;
- the lower bound of a deterministic paired 90% bootstrap interval is above
  zero;
- neither named domain regresses by more than 2% in paired median sMAPE.

Governed admission G must then satisfy all of:

- deterministic fallback when evidence is absent, malformed, incompatible,
  or locally contradicted;
- complete candidate identity, registry provenance, support, and admission
  state wherever candidate signal contributes;
- no worse than B by more than 2% overall or within either domain;
- positive paired median sMAPE improvement overall, with more wins than
  losses among channels where G actually differs from B;
- no change to an otherwise identical long-history control where the
  short-history admission path is inapplicable.

The iteration is promoted only if both S and G pass. If S fails, the Toto
candidate family receives an evidence-backed no-build decision and runtime
defaults do not change. If S passes but G fails, revise only the general
admission boundary and rerun these frozen arms; do not tune by dataset or case.

## Manual inspection and product decision

Before each stage expands, inspect at least one win, one loss, one fallback,
and one sparse-channel result. Confirm that numbers, support, identity,
revision, transfer provenance, and warnings agree across the engine artifact,
benchmark detail, and score input.

Synthetic evidence remains labelled synthetic, a smoke result cannot promote
a default, and a statistically positive average cannot excuse a broken
support or provenance contract. The checkpoint records `promote`, `revise`, or
`reject`, the user-visible consequence, retained evidence paths, remaining
risk, and the exact next action.
