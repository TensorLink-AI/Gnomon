# v0.8 D1 decision-contract decision

Decision: promote the additive sealed decision contract; retain hosted
decision-utility promotion as pending.

## Product result

Typed temporal answers now project a compact decision block into
`agent_response_contract.decisions`. It carries the canonical conclusion or
abstention, exact support and authority, selector, evidence sufficiency,
compatible interpretations and their supporting/conflicting evidence,
conditional status, interval/calibration status, decision eligibility,
automation eligibility, immutable-primary status, and artifact/question
provenance. The response contract is content-sealed and independently
recomputable.

`verify_agent_decision_selection` accepts the labelled canonical advisory
default, preserves supported binding conclusions, and permits a weak override
only when the interpretation is compatible, decision-eligible,
non-conditional, and cites evidence that the sealed packet says supports it.
It rejects invented choices, unsupported citations, conditional-only paths,
binding overrides, and abstention upgrades. Older sealed reasoning-packet
receipts retain their original rule that compatible non-conditional
interpretations are decision-eligible.

The brief temporal-answer projection now carries the evidence kinds and
calibration applicability needed to recompute that contract. No forecast,
typed conclusion, support state, context relationship, probability,
automation flag, or primary path is changed.

## Validation

The frozen 12-case label-free matrix moved from 0/12 emitted decision
contracts at baseline to 12/12 present, complete, exact, and seal-
recomputable. All twelve canonical/abstention selections verify and all twelve
invented unsupported overrides are rejected. Focused response, reasoning,
tool-surface, ContextBench, and TemporalBench integration validation passed
217 tests.

An actual brief forecast with one typed trend question produced a 10,042-byte
wire response; its sealed decision block was 1,540 bytes. It retained a weak
advisory upward conclusion, a constant opposing interpretation, their exact
evidence kinds, a calibrated interval with 37 folds, automation false, and
primary unchanged.

Historical replay over the two latest T4 rows produced six recomputable
decision contracts and preserved every canonical answer or abstention. It also
confirmed the limit: the contract cannot improve a question when the engine
does not contain the correct alternative. Therefore this result establishes a
safer and more useful agent/human boundary, not broad choice-accuracy uplift.

## External gate

The Engy credential is unavailable. The frozen real-agent matched utility,
per-property improvement, unsupported-decision, token, and latency gates were
not run. The additive projection is production-useful and does not change an
existing answer, so it remains enabled; claims of agent accuracy improvement
remain pending until that exact hosted protocol passes.
