# v0.8 categorical numeric-authority protocol

Status: frozen after rejecting the over-covering envelope treatment and before
changing the production publication boundary again.

## Question

A categorical-state mapping that fails its replay-based human recommendation
gate has not earned a distinct numeric forecast. Publishing its raw centre or
tails in the candidate portfolio invites an agent to quote unsupported
numbers, even when typed selection fields say not to choose it.

The proposed treatment makes the publication match that authority decision.
For an unadmitted governed categorical mapping, the public scenario path is
the immutable primary exactly and carries the
numeric_authority: withheld_no_distinct_path code. The source candidate remains
unchanged in the sealed dossier, and its disagreement with the primary remains
available as a diagnostic. A recurring admitted mapping remains byte-for-byte
unchanged. No support, recommendation, selection, or automation decision may
change.

## Frozen prospective matrix

The version-2 benchmarks/categoricaluncertaintybench/run.py matrix retains the
same seed 2026090205, 120 cases, four families, real dossier validator, and
real publisher used by the rejected v1 treatment. Future targets remain sealed
until scoring. The reference is the rejected envelope implementation; the
treatment changes only the numeric-authority boundary.

## Frozen gates

- all v1 completion, leakage, dossier, seal, ordering, and primary-integrity
  gates remain;
- every source candidate remains byte-identical to the reference;
- support, selection, recommendation, and automation remain identical;
- every admitted recurring path remains byte-identical;
- every unadmitted public path exactly equals the immutable primary and carries
  the explicit no-distinct-path authority code;
- aggregate WIS does not worsen, no family median worsens by more than 2%, and
  unadmitted-path mean WIS improves by at least 20%;
- aggregate coverage is within five percentage points of the nominal 80% and
  its absolute calibration error improves over the reference.

Passing permits withholding a distinct public numeric path only. It does not
discard the sealed source sensitivity, promote a candidate, improve the
primary's tier, or authorize automation.
