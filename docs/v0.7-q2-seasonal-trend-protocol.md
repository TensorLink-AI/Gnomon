# v0.7 Q2 seasonal-trend answer protocol

Status: frozen before the runner, baseline, or production implementation.

## User boundary

The requested quantity is the direction and slope of the future trend after
an admitted seasonal adjustment. It is not the raw slope of a partial
seasonal arc, a level change, or a promise that an unstable recent movement
will persist.

For an agent, the canonical direction, numeric slope, interval, support, and
automation eligibility must be mutually consistent and survive final-answer
synthesis exactly. For a human, a seasonal or regime ambiguity must be named
as the reason for advisory or abstaining output. Neither surface may change
the immutable primary forecast.

## Frozen engine corpus

Use independent seeds `7301` through `7308` for every family. Long histories
contain 240 visible points and use a 48-step untouched future. Seasonal period
is 24. Gaussian innovations are independently regenerated per family and
seed. The engine receives only the visible prefix, requested horizon, and the
declared period; generator family, seed, structural slope, and future values
remain scorer-only.

The long-history families are:

- additive seasonal upward, downward, and zero trend;
- multiplicative seasonal upward, downward, and zero trend;
- noisy non-seasonal upward, downward, and zero trend;
- an upward trend ending in a genuine plateau and a downward trend ending in
  a genuine plateau;
- positive and negative permanent level changepoints with no within-regime
  trend; and
- additive seasonal upward, downward, and zero trend where period 24 is
  visible but deliberately not admitted to the trend executable (`season=1`).

Three insufficient-cycle controls contain 30 visible points and a 12-step
future from additive seasonal upward, downward, and zero generators with
period 24 declared. They test identifiability, not short-history point
forecasting. This yields 152 frozen engine cases.

Structural slopes are `+0.08`, `-0.08`, or `0` units per step. Additive
seasonal amplitude is 8 units. Multiplicative amplitude is 8% of the local
level. Noisy non-seasonal innovation standard deviation is 1.2; other
long-history innovation standard deviation is 0.6. Plateaus begin 72 points
before the cutoff. Changepoints occur 72 points before the cutoff and shift
the level by 12 units. The runner records exact formula and case seed in its
private case row, never in the product call.

## Oracle and separate scores

The scorer estimates the realized future slope with phase fixed effects when
at least two complete future cycles exist and ordinary least squares
otherwise. It normalizes the slope by a prefix-only innovation scale so the
oracle and engine estimates use the same unit. Direction thresholds remain
the product's preregistered `-0.02` and `+0.02` normalized units. Generator
direction is retained as a structural diagnostic; it does not replace the
realized held-out oracle.

Report separately:

- product completion and future observations used by production;
- canonical direction accuracy overall and by family;
- supported-claim accuracy and coverage;
- interval coverage against the realized adjusted slope;
- support state, automation eligibility, and their exact agreement;
- slope sign and interval/direction consistency;
- unadmitted-season and insufficient-cycle abstention or advisory behavior;
- primary-forecast immutability; and
- exact agent preservation in a later API shard.

Selective utility is frozen as `+1` for a correct supported direction, `-2`
for an incorrect supported direction, `+0.25` for a correct weak/advisory
direction, `-0.25` for an incorrect weak/advisory direction, and `0` for an
abstention. Missing or failed rows remain in the denominator and score `-2`.

## Baseline and candidate discipline

First run the exact clean current head on all engine cases with resumable
append-only rows. Inspect failures by typed contract and family before any
implementation. A candidate may use only prefix-visible general evidence. It
may not branch on family, seed, expected direction, or future outcome.

The later agent-boundary shard contains at most eight representative cases
selected by family before responses are requested. It uses Evidence,
DeepSeek through Engy, `jobs=1`, bounded retries, per-call timeouts, a wall
clock limit, and resumable checkpoints. Engine completeness and exact agent
preservation are distinct scores. At least two cases also carry a context
scenario whose typed relationship is `no_distinct_numeric_path`; final agent
responses must preserve that relationship and primary immutability exactly.

## Promotion gates

Promote a general Q2 change only if the exact candidate:

- completes all 152 engine cases with zero future use and exact primary
  immutability;
- has no support or automation inflation and preserves the identity
  `automation_eligible == (support == "supported")` on every row;
- reaches at least 90% supported-direction accuracy and at least 80% supported
  interval coverage, with missing supported claims counted as failures when a
  denominator exists;
- has non-negative matched selective-utility change overall;
- improves at least one preregistered weak family and regresses no family by
  more than 2 percentage points in direction accuracy or interval coverage;
- emits zero automation-eligible answers in the unadmitted-season and
  insufficient-cycle controls;
- abstains when the requested adjusted trend is not identifiable, rather than
  substituting a level or raw partial-cycle slope;
- keeps direction, slope sign, interval, support, and automation eligibility
  internally consistent;
- preserves every canonical direction, support state, automation flag,
  `no_distinct_numeric_path` relationship, and primary-immutability fact in
  the matched agent shard; and
- passes focused tests and the complete suite isolated from machine-local TSFM
  supply.

A support-only fix may be promoted when it removes unsafe authority without
changing numerics, while a numerical mechanism that does not clear the full
matched utility and no-regression gates is retained as a documented no-build.
