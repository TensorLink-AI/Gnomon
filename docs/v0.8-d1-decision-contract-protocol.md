# v0.8 D1 decision-useful contract protocol

Status: frozen before D1 implementation.

## User boundary

An agent should receive enough typed structure to make a defensible decision,
not merely enough information to repeat a forecast fact. A human should see
the supported conclusion or explicit abstention, what supports and contradicts
it, applicable conditions, uncertainty, provenance, and whether the result is
eligible for a decision or automation.

A supported canonical conclusion remains binding. A weak conclusion is an
advisory default, not a command: another interpretation may be selected only
when it is present in Gnomon's reasoning packet and the selection cites typed
evidence that supports it. An abstained or incompatible interpretation cannot
be upgraded by presentation. The primary forecast is immutable throughout.

## Frozen diagnosis and cases

The v0.7 full TemporalBench evidence run is the historical baseline: 240/240
canonical typed answers were preserved, while official choice accuracy was
28.3% versus 30.4% for the direct-agent control. The latest two-row T4
regression had 6/6 agent-preserved canonical answers but only 3/6 canonical
answers matched official choices; a separately recorded synthesis matched 2/6
and no advisory override was accepted. These rows are diagnosis only after
this protocol and cannot be used for confirmation.

The local development contract matrix is fixed at twelve payloads crossing:

- support: `supported`, `weak`, and `abstained`;
- evidence sufficiency: `sufficient`, `mixed`, and `insufficient`;
- canonical-only, supported opposition, contradicted opposition, and
  conditional-only interpretations;
- interval present, interval absent with calibration reason, and stale or
  mismatched calibration;
- context relationship present or absent; and
- automation true, false, or omitted.

The matrix contains no benchmark answer labels. It scores projection
completeness, exactness, verifier behavior, tamper detection, support and
automation inflation, and bounded wire size.

Hosted development and confirmation use preregistered property-stratified
TemporalBench/TrendAnswerBench rows not previously scored by D1. Development
seed is `2026083601`; confirmation seed is `2026083602`. The bounded boundary
shard covers at least six questions across level, trend, volatility,
seasonality, regime, and extremes. Use Evidence, DeepSeek
`deepseek-v4-flash-0731` through Engy, temperature zero, `jobs=1`, one atomic
resumable row at a time, 180-second request timeout, 120-second tool timeout,
two bounded retries, and an explicit wall-clock limit. Missing rows remain in
denominators. If Engy is unavailable, hosted gates remain pending rather than
being substituted with a different model or a deterministic consumer.

## Candidate contract

The response projection may only reorganize already sealed Gnomon receipts.
For each typed temporal question it must emit:

- question identity, property, and inference mode;
- canonical conclusion or typed abstention and exact support;
- selector and authority (`binding`, `advisory`, or `abstained`);
- compatible interpretations with supporting and conflicting evidence kinds;
- applicable conditions and contradicted claims when present;
- interval/calibration status without manufacturing a probability;
- decision eligibility and exact automation eligibility;
- immutable-primary fact and artifact/question provenance; and
- explicit omitted counts for every bounded collection.

The contract is sealed and independently recomputable. It neither chooses a
new conclusion nor changes numbers, support, context relationship, authority,
or automation. A deterministic verifier accepts only supported citations and
provides at most one bounded repair before falling back to the labelled
canonical default.

## Measures and promotion gates

Report engine-answer completeness, response-contract completeness, exact
canonical and relationship preservation, final-agent selection, cited-evidence
validity, unsupported-decision rate, selective utility, per-property accuracy,
support/automation inflation, primary mutation, calls, tokens, latency,
retries, and wire size separately.

Promotion requires:

- 100% contract completeness and recomputation/tamper detection on the local
  matrix;
- exact canonical values, abstentions, support, relationships, conditions,
  uncertainty status, decision eligibility, automation eligibility, and
  provenance;
- zero unsupported accepted decisions, authority inflation, automation
  inflation, or primary changes;
- exact final-agent preservation of binding conclusions and abstentions;
- non-negative matched selective utility overall, improvement in at least one
  preregistered weak temporal property, and no property accuracy regression
  greater than two percentage points;
- non-increasing unsupported-decision rate for both agent submissions and a
  deterministic human-readable contract audit;
- no more than 10% p95 latency regression and no extra model request solely
  to restate a complete contract; and
- focused tests and the full suite isolated from machine-local TSFM supply.

Kill or retain the candidate as externally pending if hosted evidence cannot
establish decision benefit. A locally complete projection is not, by itself,
evidence of broad answer-quality improvement.
