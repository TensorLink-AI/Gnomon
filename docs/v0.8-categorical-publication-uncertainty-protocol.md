# v0.8 categorical publication uncertainty protocol

Status: rejected after the broader frozen GFR evaluation.

## Question

A governed categorical-state path can remain useful as a sensitivity after it
fails the recommendation gate. Its centre describes that sensitivity, but a
short or inconsistent replay does not independently calibrate its tails. The
current publisher relays those tails unchanged, allowing an unadmitted path to
look more certain than the immutable primary.

The proposed treatment preserves the candidate centre and the immutable
primary byte-for-byte. Only a governed categorical path that is ineligible for
human recommendation receives an uncertainty envelope: q10 cannot exceed the
primary q10, q90 cannot be below the primary q90, and the candidate centre must
remain inside the envelope. The result is explicitly a sensitivity envelope,
not upgraded calibration evidence. Eligible recurring candidates remain
untouched.

## Frozen prospective matrix

`benchmarks/categoricaluncertaintybench/run.py` generates 30 sealed cases in
each of four families from seed `2026090205`: one-replay-cycle stable and
reversed relationships, a recurring two-replay-cycle relationship, and an
irrelevant two-replay-cycle state schedule. Each case passes through the real
governed-candidate dossier validator and `publish_result`. Future targets are
used only after publication for scoring. Runs are serial, checkpoint every
case, and resume only when the complete run identity matches.

## Frozen gates

- 120/120 cases complete; no future target reaches any production function;
- every dossier and publication verifies and the primary is byte-preserved;
- every emitted q10/q50/q90 triple is finite and ordered;
- support, selection, recommendation, and automation decisions are unchanged;
- the source candidate centre is unchanged in every case;
- eligible recurring candidates remain byte-identical;
- every ineligible candidate exactly follows the declared primary-envelope
  rule;
- aggregate WIS does not worsen, no family median worsens by more than 2%, and
  ineligible-candidate mean WIS improves by at least 10%.

Failure preserves the existing behavior. Passing this probe supports only a
more honest uncertainty surface for unadmitted categorical sensitivities; it
does not promote candidates or widen any authority.

## Outcome

The treatment passed every gate above and reduced WIS, but the subsequently
run full GFR calibration gate exposed a defect in this protocol: it did not
penalize systematic overcoverage. Conditional-candidate coverage became 100%,
100%, and 95.8% on the three matched CiK cases, lowering the frozen candidate
calibration capability from 49.55% to 47.00%. The treatment is therefore not
the retained design. The reference and treatment artifacts remain evidence of
the rejected alternative; the numeric-authority v2 protocol supersedes it.
