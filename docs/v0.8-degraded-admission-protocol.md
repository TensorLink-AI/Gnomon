# v0.8 fold-starved structural-admission protocol

Status: frozen before changing production short-history admission.

## Question

Gnomon correctly refuses an ordinary candidate tournament when only one
disjoint full-horizon selection fold exists.  It consequently publishes
last-value even when the visible prefix contains repeated, stable evidence of
a trend or stationary level.  The proposed treatment may depart from
last-value only through a narrow, prefix-only structural screen.  It must not
turn dense overlapping origins into nominally independent folds or promote the
result above degraded support.

## Frozen prospective matrix

`benchmarks/degradedadmissionbench/run.py` generates 30 matched cases at each
of horizons 3 and 6 in six families from seed `2026091302`: stable linear
trend, stable iid level, random walk, recent level shift, recent trend
reversal, and intermittent demand.  Every case has 18 visible observations.
Only those 18 observations reach production `evaluate`; the future suffix is
sealed until the emitted path has been retained.

Run the unchanged implementation first and retain it as the reference.  Run
the treatment at the next revision with the exact seed and matrix.  Both runs
are sequential, append one JSON checkpoint per case, and are resumable.

Development of any structural thresholds uses different seeds.  The frozen
seed is not inspected until the production rule and its tests are fixed.

## Frozen gates

- 360/360 cases complete and no future target reaches the forecaster;
- all losses are finite and every result remains degraded;
- aggregate mean loss does not worsen;
- no family median loss worsens by more than 2%;
- harmful departures do not increase in random-walk, recent-shift,
  recent-reversal, or intermittent controls;
- stable-trend departure rate improves by at least 25 percentage points and
  median loss improves by at least 10%; and
- stable-level departure rate improves by at least 15 percentage points and
  median loss improves by at least 2%.

Failure preserves the last-value guardrail.  Success permits only the exact
prefix-only screen validated here; it does not weaken ordinary selection,
calibration, support, or automation gates.
