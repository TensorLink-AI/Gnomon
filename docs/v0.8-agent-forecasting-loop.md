# Gnomon v0.8 agent-forecasting improvement loop

Status: active. The durable handoff is the latest
`results/benchmark-releases/*-v08-loop-*/checkpoint.json`.

## Objective

Make Gnomon materially more useful as a forecasting tool for agents while
preserving the evidence discipline that lets humans trust it. Each issue ends
in either a validated general production improvement or a retained,
evidence-backed no-build decision. Benchmark identities, labels, answer
choices, source identities, and hidden futures may never influence runtime
behavior.

The v0.7.0 release and its artifacts are the stable comparison point. This
loop starts from integrated `main` at `9e04fcd` and does not rewrite v0.7
history.

## User outcomes

- Agents obtain a non-naive path when prefix-only evidence demonstrates
  repeatable skill, and retain a disclosed robust baseline otherwise.
- Agents discover more genuinely usable dated context without invented
  timing, magnitude, causality, or future leakage.
- Agents receive a decision-ready contract whose conclusion, contradictions,
  support, conditions, and abstentions survive final-answer synthesis.
- Humans can see why a model or context path was selected, what was rejected,
  how uncertainty was calibrated, and whether any action is safe to automate.
- Repeated runs are economical, idempotent, resumable, and explicit about
  provider failures, retries, fallbacks, and latency.
- Performance claims come from powered, complete, independently reproducible
  evaluation rather than a favorable small shard.

## Iteration discipline

Only one issue is active at a time.

1. Diagnose the smallest observed user harm and inspect actual artifacts and
   agent responses.
2. Freeze corpus, arms, denominators, seeds, metrics, uncertainty, cost,
   retries, timeouts, promotion gate, and kill criterion before implementation.
3. Reproduce on the smallest representative shard.
4. Implement one general production-useful mechanism.
5. Add contract, property, negative-path, and fault-injection tests.
6. Run matched independent validation with every failure and abstention in the
   denominator.
7. Inspect engine completeness and agent preservation separately.
8. Decide `promote`, `revise`, or `reject`; rejected mechanisms remain
   documented and disabled or removed.
9. Write and seal a resumable checkpoint, commit only intended files, and
   continue from that checkpoint after compaction.

API-driven work uses `jobs=1`, incremental result rows, bounded exponential
backoff, per-call timeouts, a wall-clock bound, and content-addressed receipts.
Local CPU evaluation may use bounded parallelism only after a small shard is
green. A GPU is not assumed.

## Invariants

- Zero future leakage and zero benchmark-identity routing.
- Context, agent synthesis, and candidate evaluation never mutate the
  immutable primary forecast.
- Last-value and seasonal-naive remain independently computed controls even
  when an optional model plugin is installed.
- Presentation never upgrades support, calibration, or automation eligibility.
- Typed relationships, including `no_distinct_numeric_path`, remain exact
  through the final agent boundary.
- Probabilities are withheld unless their calibration evidence is present and
  applicable to the requested horizon and cohort.
- Provider retry or failover never duplicates a product action and is always
  disclosed in receipts.
- Existing committed work, benchmark artifacts, and untracked files are
  preserved. `docs/astrid-btc-agent-plan.md` remains outside this loop and is
  never added to a v0.8 commit.
- `docs/v0.7-external-evaluation-intake.md` is preserved as historical intake;
  reproductions and new decisions are recorded in v0.8 artifacts.

## Issue register and frozen completion gates

### E1 — Economical and resilient orchestration

Observed gap: the retained v0.7 matched optimization preserved semantics and
resume correctness but reduced cold requests by 11.1% and warm requests by
20%, below its frozen 25% gate. Provider HTTP failures still consume visible
retries.

Product direction: deterministic extraction and canonical contract reuse;
content-addressed compilation shared across cases; request deduplication;
idempotent row receipts; retry classification and circuit breaking. Provider
failover is optional, policy-bound, and disclosed.

Promotion requires exact engine and final-agent semantic parity, exact typed
relationship preservation, zero additional leakage or fallback, 100% resume
correctness under injected interruption, no duplicate product actions, at
least 25% fewer model requests and prompt tokens on both a cold and applicable
warm matched shard, and no p95 latency regression above 10%. Compiler,
product-tool, final-agent, transport-attempt, and cache results are separate.

### F1 — Governed StatsForecast candidates

Observed gap: Gnomon still publishes last-value-like paths frequently; the
v0.7 numeric selector family did not clear its prospective gate.

Product direction: an optional `statsforecast` extra and soft-loaded adapter
for a small preregistered family: AutoETS, AutoARIMA, AutoTheta, and a suitable
intermittent-demand model. MSTL is deferred until histories with multiple
complete seasonal cycles are independently represented. Missing or broken
optional dependencies remain a disclosed soft skip, not a runtime failure.

Every candidate is evaluated by Gnomon's outer prefix-only rolling-origin
contest against last-value and, when eligible, seasonal-naive. Internal model
search is one family representative, not multiple uncorrected admissions.
Version, model identity, folds, scores, failures, selection evidence, and
rejection reason are recorded.

Promotion requires at least 99% completion, no silent fallback, at least 20
admitted departures, at least 70% precision among departures, positive paired
median bounded relative MAE gain, more wins than losses, a non-negative
deterministic 90% paired-bootstrap lower bound, and no preregistered family
median regression beyond 2%. A fresh naturalistic confirmation must be
non-inferior before any candidate becomes enabled by default when installed.
The dependency-free base installation must remain unchanged.

### C1 — Context recall at fixed safety

Observed gap: the retained 112-case v0.7 structural stress run achieved zero
leakage and 100% empirical admission precision but only 45.833% recall.

Product direction: broaden proposal supply with deterministic temporal entity,
event alias, coreference, and relative-date resolution; optional retrieval may
propose additional spans, but the existing strict admission boundary remains
authoritative. Proposal recall, engine contract completeness, admitted numeric
recall, conditional-scenario recall, and agent preservation are distinct.

Promotion requires 100% empirical admission precision, zero leakage, exact
primary immutability, exact typed-relationship preservation, and a
statistically positive recall increase on frozen fresh generator seeds and
naturalistic documents. Scenario-only context is never counted as an admitted
numeric effect, and invented effect magnitude remains zero.

### D1 — Decision-useful typed contracts

Observed gap: canonical typed facts survive synthesis, but preservation alone
does not consistently improve the agent's downstream choice or action.

Product direction: a general decision contract containing supported
conclusion, typed value or abstention, contradicted claims, applicable
conditions, support, uncertainty, decision eligibility, automation
eligibility, and provenance. It represents claims rather than benchmark
options and never consumes answer labels as product inputs.

Promotion requires exact canonical and relationship preservation, no primary
change, no support or automation inflation, non-negative matched selective
utility overall, improvement in at least one preregistered weak temporal
property, no property regression beyond 2 percentage points, and lower or
equal unsupported-decision rate for both agents and humans.

### A1 — Calibrated uncertainty and risk-tiered automation

Observed gap: probabilities are often withheld and useful alternatives remain
human-review-only because applicable prospective calibration evidence is
insufficient.

Product direction: cohort- and horizon-matched residual calibration, including
distribution-free intervals where data suffices, plus explicit action tiers:
`advisory`, `reversible_low_impact`, and `high_impact`. v0.8 may graduate only
the first two; high-impact automation remains false.

Promotion requires complete calibration lineage, no interval inversion,
non-worsening proper score, empirical interval coverage within a
preregistered tolerance on untouched data, non-increasing false-action cost,
and positive selective utility at the proposed coverage. Missing, stale, or
mismatched calibration must fail closed. Any automation graduation also needs
a clean prospective confirmation after the policy is frozen.

### V1 — Broader independent evaluation

The final candidate is evaluated as a pyramid rather than one crash-prone
matrix:

- at least 500 deterministic local cases spanning level, trend, seasonal,
  phase-unstable, intermittent, regime-shift, spike, heteroskedastic, and
  short-history families across multiple horizons and frequencies;
- at least 100 untouched naturalistic/public-series cases with frozen cutoffs;
- at least 24 independently seeded agent-boundary cases accumulated in small
  resumable shards across ContextBench and TemporalBench;
- at least 12 fresh CiK cases when the external harness and provider are
  available, otherwise a documented external-evaluation blocker rather than a
  substituted claim.

Final reporting separates forecast skill, interval calibration, context
supply/admission, typed-contract completeness, agent preservation, decision
utility, topology cost, retries, latency, and failures. Every row stays in its
denominator. The full isolated repository suite and exact-head CI run only
after all small shards are green. Completion yields a release recommendation
or a no-release decision; it does not itself authorize a tag or publication.
