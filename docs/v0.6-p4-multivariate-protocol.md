# V0.6 P4 covariate and multivariate protocol

Status: frozen before production changes on 2026-08-30.

## User hypothesis

When an agent supplies aligned related series, Gnomon should consider them
through a real, validated adapter boundary and publish a cross-series forecast
only when it beats the strongest univariate baseline on identical historical
folds. The agent and human should be able to see which series were used, why
the candidate won or lost, and whether the published primary changed.

This iteration does not claim that every upstream model which can theoretically
accept multiple channels is multivariate in Gnomon. A capability is advertised
only when Gnomon's invoked adapter method actually accepts and validates the
corresponding inputs.

## Audit finding and bounded change

The built-in guarded VAR(1) candidate already refits at each fold origin and
competes with univariate candidates under the common admission margin. It is,
however, a bespoke predictor outside `ForecastAdapter`, and its public gate
does not name the related series used. Separately, the TSFM capability matrix
marks Toto 2.0 and TTM as supporting multivariate targets even though their
Gnomon adapter methods receive only one `history` vector.

The treatment may only:

1. put the existing VAR calculation behind the model-neutral adapter request,
2. validate aligned `related_series` and declare them in result metadata,
3. expose the adapter/protocol identity and retained series in gate evidence,
4. remove TSFM multivariate capability claims that the invoked protocol cannot
   honor.

The estimator, minimum correlation, common evaluation folds, admission margin,
and univariate candidates remain unchanged. Native multivariate TSFM inference
is a no-build for this iteration unless a pinned local dependency proves the
same contract without changing the frozen corpus or gate.

## Frozen prospective corpus

The retained local benchmark contains 12 hourly, two-series cases with 192
training observations and a 12-step future holdout. Cases are generated once
from a checked-in deterministic generator with seeds 4100 through 4111:

- six `lagged_driver` cases in which the target has a stable, lagged dependency
  on the other observed target series;
- three `shared_pattern_control` cases whose correlation is visible but whose
  target future is adequately forecast from its own history;
- three `independent_control` cases with no useful cross-series relationship.

Neither case family nor seed is visible to runtime code. Both arms receive the
same training prefix. Control runs omit multivariate candidacy; treatment runs
enable it. Published points are scored against the untouched final holdout with
sMAPE. Selection scores and gate records come only from rolling origins inside
the training prefix.

## Frozen gates

All missing, failed, non-finite, or incomplete cases remain in denominators.
The P4 candidate is promoted only if every gate passes:

- **Completion:** 12/12 control and 12/12 treatment forecasts complete locally.
- **Protocol:** the VAR adapter passes exact-horizon, finite-output,
  deterministic-replay, immutable-input, variable-horizon, related-series
  alignment, and unsupported-feature rejection contract tests.
- **Identical folds:** every eligible treatment result records a VAR score in
  the same selection-score table as its univariate competitors; no separate
  validation window may decide admission.
- **Prospective skill:** at least four of six lagged-driver targets admit VAR,
  their paired median holdout sMAPE improvement over control is at least 10%,
  and admitted driver cases have positive aggregate improvement.
- **Control safety:** no independent control admits VAR; across all six control
  cases the treatment median holdout sMAPE is non-inferior within 2 percentage
  points and no admitted control is worse by more than 2 percentage points.
- **Provenance:** every eligible gate names the target, all retained related
  series, adapter kind/name, protocol version, and strongest correlation.
- **Leakage:** corrupting any target or related-series value after a fold origin
  changes neither that origin's forecast nor its admission inputs.
- **Fallback:** ineligible or losing multivariate candidates preserve a
  supported univariate primary and disclose the deciding check.
- **Capability truth:** no TSFM advertises multivariate targets unless the
  method invoked by Gnomon accepts a validated related-series request.
- **Numerical parity:** moving VAR behind the adapter boundary changes neither
  its fold predictions nor its final predictions beyond `1e-9` absolute error.

## Execution and inspection

This benchmark is local and runs serially. Each case is written atomically to
its own resumable result row; retries are limited to one and each case has a
60-second timeout. The release record retains the evaluated commit, dirty-tree
state, generator version, seeds, per-case points/errors/admission, gate
evidence, summary denominators, and file hashes.

Actual artifacts for one admitted driver, one losing correlated control, and
one ineligible independent control are inspected manually. Agent-boundary
probing is required only if the public decision relationship changes; otherwise
P3's preservation contract remains the invariant and this iteration records
engine completeness separately.

## Kill and no-build rules

Reject or revise the production change if it weakens point-in-time slicing,
changes the estimator while measuring the boundary refactor, silently consumes
an undeclared series, suppresses a univariate fallback, or clears skill by
tuning thresholds after seeing holdouts. A native TSFM path reaches a
documented no-build when its installed Gnomon method cannot accept the feature,
its weights/dependency are unavailable under the frozen resource budget, or it
fails the adapter contract; its capability flag remains false.
