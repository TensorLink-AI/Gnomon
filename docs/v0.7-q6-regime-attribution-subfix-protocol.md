# v0.7 Q6 regime attribution and rebound subfix protocol

Status: frozen after the exact current-head baseline at `1b2e479`, before
production implementation or candidate scoring.

## Baseline diagnosis

All 24 cases completed on the investigation, unlabelled anomaly, and labelled
anomaly surfaces with deterministic replay. All eight planted permanent
shifts were correctly classified as supported regime shifts at index 72, and
all 16 planted point events were recalled on every surface.

Two general boundary defects reproduced:

- investigation computes a global robust point score independently of its
  own supported regime result. It therefore reports all 48 observations after
  each shift as separate anomalies: 384 false events and 4% pooled event
  precision despite exact changepoint detection;
- when synthetic grading selects the one-step forecast-interval detector, an
  extreme observation enters the next fit unchanged. Five rows flag the
  ordinary observation at index 73 as a second event after the spike at index
  72. Unlabelled pooled event precision is 27.12% at 100% recall.

Labelled selection exposes `label_f1`, has no rebound duplicate, and reaches
44.44% pooled precision at 100% recall. Investigation preserves both nearby
events exactly. These green behaviors remain regression controls.

## Frozen candidate mechanisms

For investigation only, use its already-computed, supported `regime_shift`
edges as descriptive attribution boundaries. Score point anomalies within
the resulting regimes rather than against a global centre. Do not split on an
undetermined or transient edge. Return the final anomaly list plus a compact
typed attribution record containing boundary indices, raw and final counts,
suppressed count, and the relationship `explained_by_regime_shift`. This may
reclassify repeated points; it may not change the changepoint list,
classification, onset, or support.

For the generic one-step forecast-interval detector, keep a causal cleaned
history for detector state. Once enough prior residuals exist, an extreme
innovation remains scored at its original magnitude but enters subsequent
fits as its one-step prediction rather than the extreme observation. The
cleaning threshold is fixed from prior residuals only. It cannot inspect a
future observation, supplied label, case identity, detector outcome, or
expected event count. Other detector formulas and the existing labelled or
synthetic selection contest remain unchanged.

## Subfix gates

Against the identical 24 frozen cases, promote only if:

- all baseline-complete rows and deterministic replays remain complete;
- all eight baseline-correct shift classifications and strongest
  changepoints remain exact;
- investigation post-shift duplicate alerts fall from 384 to zero and every
  suppression is disclosed by the typed regime-attribution record;
- event recall remains 100% on all three surfaces and within every planted
  point-event family;
- investigation and unlabelled event precision both improve, while labelled
  precision does not fall by more than two percentage points;
- rebound duplicates fall from five to zero without suppressing either
  nearby genuine event on any baseline-correct row;
- labelled and unlabelled selection-basis fields remain exact; and
- focused tests and the full TSFM-isolated suite pass.

If causal cleaning does not remove rebound duplicates without a recall or
nearby-event regression, reject that mechanism independently; the regime
attribution fix may still be promoted on its own gates.
