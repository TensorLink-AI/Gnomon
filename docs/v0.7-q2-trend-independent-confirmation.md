# v0.7 Q2 trend independent confirmation

Status: frozen after the exact candidate at `a3dc093`, before running any
confirmation row.

## Purpose

The development matrix shows a useful weak/advisory improvement, but its
240-point history and 48-step horizon provide only two disjoint origins. It
cannot measure supported-claim accuracy or supported interval coverage. This
confirmation keeps that absence visible instead of weakening the minimum-fold
rule or treating weak answers as supported.

## Matched arms

Run the unchanged pre-candidate product at `11b8b48` and the exact candidate at
`a3dc093` through the same harnesses.

- TrendAnswerBench uses new seeds `8121` through `8128` across the same 19
  frozen families, formulas, lengths, horizons, and scoring rules. Seeds are
  replaced before case construction; no runner or product code changes.
- PropertyBench uses new top-level seeds `8301` and `8317`, 20 replicates each.
  Its existing trend lane supplies long histories and enough rolling origins
  to score supported direction and interval coverage. All other properties
  remain regression diagnostics and may not be hidden from the artifact.

Both arms run from detached temporary worktrees. Raw results, exact revisions,
manifests, exit status, and every row are retained. A failed suite gate does
not erase property-specific evidence.

## Confirmation gates

The candidate confirms only if:

- both TrendAnswerBench arms complete all 152 cases with zero future use and
  exact input/primary immutability;
- candidate matched selective utility is non-negative overall and no lower
  than control;
- candidate identifiable-direction accuracy is no lower overall and no
  non-seasonal family falls by more than 2 percentage points;
- all insufficient-cycle rows abstain and unadmitted/insufficient automation
  remains zero;
- canonical direction/estimate inconsistency and calibrated weak zero-width
  intervals remain zero;
- pooled PropertyBench supported trend direction accuracy is at least 90%,
  supported interval coverage is at least 80%, and neither is lower than
  control by more than 2 percentage points;
- PropertyBench trend best-estimate accuracy does not fall by more than 2
  percentage points; and
- no non-trend PropertyBench property changes between arms, apart from fields
  whose aggregate status is mechanically affected by the trend rows.

Passing confirms a general trend executable for its published support tiers.
It does not convert the development matrix's weak answers into supported
claims, excuse the unadmitted-season raw-accuracy regression caused by safe
abstention, or supersede the later exact agent-preservation shard.
