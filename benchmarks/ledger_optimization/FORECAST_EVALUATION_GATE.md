# Gate for another forecast-error evaluation

This is an evaluation-design constraint after negative confirmation 010 and
development audit 013, not a fresh final manifest or authorization to inspect a
new final set. The target remains at least 20% lower mean per-case RMSLE than an
equally informed no-ledger agent, with the paired 95% interval excluding zero
and improvement over 1.1.9. Workflow token savings do not satisfy it.

## Select the task before measuring the advantage

A subsequent benchmark must represent recurring forecast decisions with
observable forecast-time context and matured outcomes that can recur in later
decisions. Define the population, operational horizon, time span, inclusion rules
and recording/source assumptions based on the task. Fix sampling and split seeds
before calculating candidate errors. Do not filter series or origins by which
arm wins, provider disagreement, realized oracle gain or a causal story inferred
from later demand. Genuine publication/revision timestamps are preferable; any
synthetic availability policy must be shared and explicit.

Keep the same forecasting capabilities and recipes in every arm. Do not replace
forecast selection with calibration, ensembles, newly fitted model families or
business-cost scoring. A source or task amendment must be recorded separately
with its substantive justification. A failed benchmark may reveal limited
opportunity; it is not permission to manufacture an easy control.

## Development gates before another paid confirmation

1. Establish an independent data split and freeze its provenance. Develop only
   on the development portion. Reserve final data before outcome analysis and
   prevent the diagnostic entry point from accepting its hash.
2. Reproduce candidate metrics independently. Report per-case hindsight and
   constant-per-series hindsight diagnostics against current CV. Label both as
   future-aware bounds and label CV as a deterministic reference. If even
   hindsight cannot reach 20% on development, do not spend on repeated selection
   tuning there or conceal that finding by deleting cases.
3. Show a past-only development policy that improves on both current CV and the
   incumbent support rule, with all cold starts included. Use deliberate
   perturbations to verify that future data cannot change current decisions.
   Report evidence-linkage negative controls separately; corrupted information
   must never become the no-ledger arm. A positive diagnostic is not sufficient
   to establish agent advantage or to claim confidence.
4. Freeze one candidate for a full matched development agent run. Give all arms
   the same raw events, model/tool capabilities, persistence opportunities,
   forecast and correction budgets, outcome visibility and authoritative typed
   completion handling. The treatment is organization/retrieval of accumulated
   evidence, not privileged information or stricter parsing for the control.
   Record every API attempt and unavailable billing information.
5. Require a credible development result before opening another final cohort:
   the complete matched run must reach the unchanged point target and improve
   over 1.1.9, with no unresolved numerical, visibility, fairness or accounting
   failures. This is a spending gate, not independent evidence of superiority.
   Freeze source, prompts, budgets, seeds, sample size and the shared temporal
   block/series uncertainty calculation before final dispatch.
6. Run the entire untouched final grid once. Preserve cold starts, failures,
   fallbacks and exclusions under the same predeclared rules. The final point
   estimate and interval, plus provenance/fairness audits, decide success. A
   failed result remains failed; no after-the-fact resampling of the population.

There is currently no passing candidate under these gates. Screens 011 and 012
failed to improve incumbent support. Audit 013 shows real historical-linkage
sensitivity but insufficient headroom on existing development cases. Confirmation
010 remains spent and negative. The separate experience-workflow and Hermes
experiments cannot be presented as new confirmation of this objective.
